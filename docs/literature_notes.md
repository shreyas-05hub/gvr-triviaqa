## 2607.25600 — "Beyond Self-Knowledge" (Sah et al.)
*Logged 2026-10-07 at PI request. Details are from an HTML summary; **PI to verify against the full text before writing.***

- Gates **retrieval** with **verbalized confidence** on **closed** models, with a **count-matched random control**.
- Implication for this study: a gated-retrieval-vs-random result is **not novel by itself**.
- Our novelty claim is limited to: **self-verification vs retrieval under one logit-based gate**, on an **open-weight** model, **cost-accounted**.
